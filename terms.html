afterwork_analytics: Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-10-09

afterwork_analytics is a personal, single-user tool. It is operated by the owner of the TikTok account @afterwork_collector, for that owner's own use only. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the tool does

With the owner's authorization through TikTok Login Kit, the tool reads the owner's own public video statistics (view, like, comment and share counts) and account counts (followers, following, likes, videos) through the TikTok Display API, to track the performance of the owner's own channel.

The tool is used only by its owner, only on the owner's own account, and in line with the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok for Developers Terms. It does not post, edit or delete anything, and does not contact other TikTok users.
