afterwork_analytics: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

afterwork_analytics is a personal, single-user tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @afterwork_collector. No other person's data is collected.

Data accessed

After the owner authorizes the app through TikTok Login Kit, the tool reads: the account's basic profile identifiers (open ID, display name), account counts (followers, following, likes, videos), and, for the owner's public videos, the title, duration, creation time and view, like, comment and share counts.

How it is used and stored

The data is used only to keep a private record of the owner's own channel performance. It is stored as files on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are kept in a local file on that computer and are never published.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared, disclosed or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising or to train any model.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete the stored files and revoke the app's access at any time from the TikTok app (Settings, Security and permissions, Apps and services). Revoking access stops all further reads.

Contact

Message @afterwork_collector on TikTok.
